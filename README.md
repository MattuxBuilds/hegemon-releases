# HEGEMON

A grand-strategy game set in the Hellenistic world, 280 to 146 BC: a turn-based campaign map and real-time 3D battles. What you decide on the campaign map (supply, scouting, walls, generals, ground and season, forced marches) shows up on the battlefield.

This is an **unfinished playtest build**. Expect rough edges. There is no sound yet.

## Download and run

1. Go to [Releases](../../releases/latest) and download `Hegemon-playtest.zip`.
2. Unzip the whole `Hegemon` folder somewhere (the Desktop is fine). Do not run it from inside the zip.
3. Double-click `Hegemon.exe`.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the build is unsigned: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**You need:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A graphics card around a GTX 1660 Ti or better for smooth battles. Nothing to install.

## New here?

Choose **Tutorial** on the main menu. It has a short guided battle, then the first turns of a campaign as Rome. **How to play** explains everything else and opens from any menu.

## What is in this build

- Six playable powers on a 257-province map of the Mediterranean and the East.
- Real-time battles with thousands of soldiers: formations, flanks, missiles, morale, generals, sieges and walls.
- Supply lines, fog of war, scouting, ambushes and mercenaries.
- Generals and families with traits and loyalty.
- An economy of buildings, goods and trade routes.
- People: four classes with approval, tax levels, policies, unrest and revolts.

## Feedback

Please open an [Issue](../../issues). What helps most:

- What you were doing, and what you expected to happen instead.
- A screenshot if something looks wrong.
- Anything confusing: what did you try to do and could not work out how?
- What was fun, and where you got bored.

If the game crashes or will not start, run `Hegemon.console.exe` instead. It opens a text window with the log; copy or screenshot the last lines into your Issue.
